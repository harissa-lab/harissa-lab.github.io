Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

harissa-lab ("the developer"), the developer of "MP3 Leveler" (Japanese name: MP3オトソロエ, "the app"), sets out how user information is handled in the app as follows.

1. Music files

  • The app reads MP3 files in the folder you choose and analyzes and adjusts their volume.
  • All analysis and adjustment are done only on your device. Music files and their contents are never sent outside your device, such as to external servers. The developer never receives or stores your music files.
  • Adjusted files are saved as new files in the same folder as the originals. Files are deleted only when you select them and confirm.
  • The app can only access the folder you choose.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following information only on your device and does not send it to the developer. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

  • The last folder you chose and app settings (match level, prevent clipping, language, etc.)
  • Analysis results (loudness, peak and length of each file, to skip re-analysis)
  • Ad display count (used to decide when to show full-screen ads)

3. Advertising

  • The app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show ads.
  • Google AdMob may collect information such as the advertising ID, device type and OS version, IP address, and ad views and taps, for ad delivery and measurement. Music files and their contents are never used for advertising.
  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and certain other regions are shown a consent form for ads. You can change your choice from Settings → "Ad consent settings" (shown only in regions where it is required).
  • For how Google handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
  • You can turn off personalized ads in your device settings.
    Android: Settings > Google > Ads

4. Disclosure to third parties

The developer does not provide user information to third parties except as required by law. For ad delivery under Section 3, Google handles information in accordance with its own terms.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children. If a child under 13 uses the app, a parent or guardian should review this policy and use the app together with the child.

6. Changes to this policy

The developer may change this policy as needed. Changes take effect when posted in the app or on a web page designated by the developer.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, please use the contact information on the app's Google Play store page.
