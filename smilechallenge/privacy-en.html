Privacy Policy

Established: October 21, 2026 / Last updated: November 5, 2026

harissa-lab ("the developer"), the developer of "Smile Challenge!" ("the app"), sets out how user information is handled in the app as follows.

1. Camera video

  • The app uses the front camera to detect facial expressions and movements.
  • All analysis of the camera video (face position and expression detection) is done only on your device.
  • Camera video is never sent outside your device, including to any server. The developer never receives or stores camera video or face information.
  • Face detection data is used only temporarily for detection and is not stored on the device (except what you choose to save in "Progress" below).

2. Play videos

  • If "Record" is on in Settings, the screen is recorded as a video during play. Videos are created only on your device.
  • Videos include game sound effects and music. Only if "Record voice" is on, microphone audio (such as your voice) is also included. The microphone is used only while recording, and audio is not sent outside your device.
  • Videos are passed to a destination you choose only when you tap "Share" (posting to SNS or messaging apps) or "Save".
  • Videos shared to other services are handled under those services' terms and privacy policies. Please check the content before posting.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following information only on your device. It is not sent to the developer's servers and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

  • App settings (play time, teacher type and color, accessories, sound on/off, language, etc.)
  • Play records (days played, total play time, best scores, play count)
  • Daily reminder on/off and time (reminders are scheduled on the device and nothing is sent outside)
  • Photos taken in "Progress" (neutral and smile) and mouth-corner height values (only those you choose to save; stored only on the device and never sent outside; deleted with "Delete this photo" in Progress or by uninstalling the app)

4. Disclosure to third parties

The developer does not provide user information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Children

The app is not made for children, but families can enjoy it together. If a child under 13 uses the app, a parent or guardian should review this policy and use the app together with the child.

6. Changes to this policy

The developer may change this policy as needed. Changes take effect when posted in the app or on a web page designated by the developer.

7. Contact

Please send feedback or questions about this policy or the app via "Feedback / Contact" in the app's Settings.

  • The information sent is: the type and content of your message, your reply email (only if entered), the app version, device model, OS version and screen size.
  • Messages reach the developer via Google Forms, provided by Google LLC. For how Google handles information, see Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Information you send is used only to respond to you and to improve the app, and is not provided to third parties (except as required by law).
