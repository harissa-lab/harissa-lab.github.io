DJ Asobi Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026 / Last updated: November 17, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android app "DJ Asobi" (DJあそび, the "App") handles user information.

1. Information collected

The developer of the App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location. The App has no account registration or login.

However, the App uses Google AdMob to show ads. For showing ads, measuring their performance and preventing fraud, AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, your IP address, and information about your device and app usage (see "4. Ads").

2. Music files on your device

The App reads only the music files in the folders or files you choose with the standard Android file picker.

Music files are processed only on your device for playback, scratching and tempo (BPM) analysis.

Analysis results (title, length, BPM, beat positions and waveform outline), the chosen folder, and the state when you last closed the App (songs, positions, speeds, looks, drum set settings and display language) are saved in the App's private storage on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the App and are never backed up externally.

The App includes songs made by the developer with Google Gemini (AI music generation). They are copied to the same private storage on first launch.

3. Data sharing

The App never sends your music files or analysis results outside your device.

The ad-related information described in "1. Information collected" is sent to AdMob (Google). The developer does not receive, share or sell this information.

4. Ads

The App shows Google AdMob ads in the song list and when you change the look of a record (by watching an ad). Ads are never shown on the playing screen.

For how AdMob handles information, see Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Depending on your region, a consent screen about the use of your information may be shown before ads.

You can reset or turn off the advertising ID for personalized ads in your device settings (for example, Android Settings > Privacy > Ads).

The App does not use any analytics tools.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not designed for children, and the developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about the App or this policy, please use the developer contact (email address) shown on the App's Google Play page.
